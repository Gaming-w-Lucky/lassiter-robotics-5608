Team 5608 — 2026–27

Meet the Trojans.

Lassiter Robotics is FIRST Robotics Competition Team 5608 from Lassiter High School in Marietta, Georgia. Students design, manufacture, wire, program, drive, and support competition robots while learning how engineering works under real pressure.

Designers. Programmers. Builders. Drivers. Leaders. One team.

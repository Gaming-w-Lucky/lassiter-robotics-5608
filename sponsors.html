Become a sponsor

Sponsorship runs through Lassiter Robotics Boosters, Inc., a 501(c)(3) public charity, so gifts are tax-deductible. Levels start at $100, and larger sponsors get their logo on the competition robot, pit banners, team shirts and this website.

Questions about sponsoring: board@5608boosters.org

Other ways to help

Employer matchMany companies match employee gifts dollar for dollar. Ask your HR team.
In-kindTools, materials, machining time and shop equipment count toward a sponsorship level.
VolunteerMentor a subteam, chaperone a trip, or help run the pit on competition days.
Spirit shopTeam shirts, hoodies and hats. Proceeds go to the build budget.

All of these run through 5608boosters.org.

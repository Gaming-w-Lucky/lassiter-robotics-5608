The robot

Built to lock the grid.

01 · Drivetrain
Four-module swerve drive with TalonFX drive and steer motors, CANcoders, and a Pigeon 2 gyro.
02 · Intake
Roller intake on a motorized pivot that drops down to collect Fuel and stows back up.
03 · Indexer
A spindexer that feeds Fuel into the shooter.
04 · Shooter
Sets its speed from the robot's distance to the hub, using a tuned distance-to-speed table.
05 · Endgame
Hang mechanism driven by a REV SPARK MAX.
06 · Vision
Front and back Limelight cameras find the robot's position on the field from AprilTags.
07 · Software
Java and WPILib command-based, CTRE Phoenix 6 swerve, and PathPlanner autonomous routines.

The game

REBUILT.

REBUILT was the 2026 FIRST Robotics Competition game. Two alliances of three robots race to collect foam balls called Fuel and score them into their hub, then finish the match by climbing the Tower for bonus points.

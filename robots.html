Team 5608's 2026 robot

GridLocker played the 2026 game, REBUILT, at the PCH District events in Dalton and Columbus, the Peachtree District Championship in Macon, and the GRITS offseason event. It picked up the Team Spirit Award in Dalton and the Rising All-Star Award at the District Championship.

How it works

Pulled from the 2026 robot code. Weights, dimensions and gear details will be added by the build team.

DrivetrainFour-module swerve. TalonFX drive and steer motors, CANcoders, Pigeon 2 gyro.
IntakeRollers on a motorized pivot. Drops to collect Fuel, folds back up to travel.
IndexerA spindexer that lines Fuel up and feeds the shooter.
ShooterPicks its speed from the robot's distance to the hub, using a tuned distance-to-speed table.
EndgameHang mechanism on a REV SPARK MAX.
VisionTwo Limelights, front and back, locate the robot from the field's AprilTags.
SoftwareJava, WPILib command-based, CTRE Phoenix 6 swerve, PathPlanner autos.

On the field

The game: REBUILT

REBUILT was the 2026 FIRST Robotics Competition game. Two alliances of three robots collect foam balls called Fuel and score them in their hub, then climb the Tower at the end of the match for extra points.
